Every day Ill create a *daily note* that follows the [[Daily Note Template]]. This template is designed to tie my DOING and my THINKING. 

The logic goes that if I am able to come up with ideas during the day I will write them down in the day's improvements (for things currently going on) or reflections (anything else). This way I can then link them to the actual repository of ideas I hold.

I have also installed the *calendar* and *reminder* plugins so that task management is directly integrated into the daily notes system. Daily notes can reference to concept or project notes as needed. 

#### Weekly Notes:
Weekly notes are intended to help me keep track of overarching goals/projects that I got to do every week. This could be [[Habit Tracking Template|habit tracking]], to-dos, or reading lists.
