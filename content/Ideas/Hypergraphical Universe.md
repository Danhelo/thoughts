From what I understand, it refers to the idea that the universe (and everything) can be conceived fundamentally from the angle of computer science and not physics. In fact, physics is an approximation of the following train of thought.

First, the only true thing that exists is information. Information IS everything. The way information changes and interacts with other information is computation, but computation is just information acting on other information.

Therefore we can say the universe is one huge informational system performing computations. We can represent such a system through a [[hypergraph]]. The logic goes that such a hypergraph need not have many complex "base rules" to seem like an incredibly complex system to us (a piece of information in the graph), kind of like in conway's game of life. 

In fact, wolfram's physics project seeks to figure out these fundamental rules. Such rules would be approximated by models like General Relativity or Quantum Physics. A theory of everything would understand these rules.

Then, our consciousness, emotions, and all the quirky soft shit we humans experience can be explained as an informational layer in the hypergraph. Are emotions are REAL in the sense that they ARE information, however they exist only as states in the subjective computational states of our minds (another informational collective). In other words, while they are real, they are not objective, in that other minds can't interact with them directly.

In fact, such is the case for everything. The world we as humans see is within our minds, it is an informational construct (call it representation) of the information that goes in as input into our computers (brains). Such inputs then get processed into the "game engine" representation of our consciousness, however this is a subject for [[Computational Theory of Mind]].
