# **New social order**

Create a new sort of “capital” powered by technology. It would be an [[altruist currency]] where people gain “money” (this new currency) by virtue of how they help others get this money.

Variant of this idea (or is it the same but better? IDK): [[Marketism]]. 

Basically the currency would have a multiplier on how others use yours to make more. This could only be powered by a blockchain tracking whose money is helping whose else.

Compile arguments that lean into natural human incentives. Capitalism assumes it’s all individualistic. Social/communism idealizes the altruistic side.

Reality lies, as always, somewhere in the middle. We need to create a system where selfish altruism is incentivized.

“Money” should quantify more than just material value. Tech could help create a utility market where people can rate the amount of fulfillment/happiness that others tokens brought into the fold. In fact, tokens could signify friendship, time, compassion or wisdom.

As explained by capitalist realism, any capitalist criticism will be co opted by the system. This happened with crypto’s original promise of financial liberation. Really, the only way to combat this is to rely on meta-irony (as is increasingly being shown by art).

Meta ironic ideas are resistant to capitalist realism’s materialistic transformation. The new system would have to start as a sort of “Trojan-horse” where its interpretation as a new means of making money and of starting a revolution are equally valid.

In other words, it is sold as the next big system, the next revolution, while likely acting as yet another capitalist fad and cash grab. However, in the fact that the theory is aware of this meta ironic quality, it can transcend capitalisms force on it, as slowly more people realize how vacuous their attacks are. Look at what [[MSCHF]] is doing for contemporary art as an example and adapt that to an economic ideology.

Also fundamentally you have to explain why such a system combats both capitalism (traditional), socialism’s, and communism’s failures, while providing a new perspective.

Key takeaway is that a cure to capitalist realism has to be a meta ironic idea, at least during the transition state.

Lastly, all policy concerning the implementation, change, and validity of the new “capital” has to be decentralized and democratic.

May God help us all. I hope people become less stupid soon or we are screwed. Capitalism and its idiot (literal use of the word) will take us and the whole planet down with them.

In order to avoid a “Nosedive” situation where people are only altruistic superficially but really they just want to maximize their score, your personal “capital” amounts have to be obfuscated and reduced to impact made nothing more. This or have it also be a [[meta irony]]


## On the work culture:
Saw a video on how [[Assumed Ambition]], the capitalist tendency to assume people only want menial jobs temporarily in a reinforcing loop of no experience = bad jobs, is related to an interesting contradiction in capitalism.

Basically, capitalism does not actually optimize for efficiency in markets under all cases. In fact it trades it for anything that reinforces the system itself. Why do we segment the market with goods and bads (shitty products), if making something once by a passionate artisan is cheaper in the long run and also more fulfilling to the worker?

Sidenote: It would be more efficient if we all worked less (which we can thanks to technology) but capitalist culture creates an expectation for us to overwork ourselves in the pursuit of artificial ambition. The perfect capitalist subject is someone who is ambitious enough to step on other's toes and externalizes their needs onto consumption objects --- exactly what you see happening in our culture. 

Well the system has an incentive to make the worker expendable in the pursuit of profit, but beyond that, to install a culture where working cannot be meaningful. It has to separate it to create extra consumption need, and extra work supply ((lowering price to maximize profit for capital holders), like how parents cant be with their children so they compensate by buying more stuff).

A patch to capitalism needs to value the passion/quality behind a product more, not just price, since cheapness has hidden societal, personal, and economic long tem costs. Culturally, there needs to be an emphasis on dignifying menial work since it is that precise undignification that reinforces capitalist exploitation of those positions, not an ACTUAL economic need. Aka, service workers need not do their jobs poorly but culture expects they do so they do and thus they get paid minimum wage and thus they do and thus culture expects it to be the case.

The altruist currency must weigh that passion so that people are placed into the passionate positions (including menial ones).
