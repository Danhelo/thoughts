#economics/finance

It started with Newton and some guys in the 1800s trying to price options. They used [[Expected Value]] to match buyers and sellers.

Then came modernity and the infamous [[Black-Scholes-Merton Equation]] that factored in drift and not just traditional [[Hedging]]. 
All based on the [[Markov Models (Decision Proceses)]] and general [[Stochastic Calculus]] that was developed by [[RenTech]] some time ago.

Read the [[Jim Simons Book]] or some shit that should be fun.
