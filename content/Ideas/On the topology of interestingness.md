#philosophy 

Make an LLM / Vectorizer powered program that quantifies how “crazy” / schizo a sentence is, as a function of the “unexpectedness” of the token elements as in never-before-seen combinations. It would then normalize the space with a metric of coherence.

  

this is probably how interesting ideas are generated. exploring tail end distributions of what has been thought up, while constricting the search to specific coherence criteria. hence why they have to be at the limit of one (or more) fields.

  

however this also means there’s a combinatorial explosion of interesting ideas not at the edges of the search, with lesser coherence constraints. This is probably what fiction is. 

  

In that case, why is fiction archetypal? The topology of coherence cannot be too forgiving, with “meaning wells” that have wide (and possibly) deep pits. Hence fiction becomes the retracing of paths through meaningspace in different ways.

  

Much like how in relativity a massful object bends spacetime around it so that paths follow the curvature, so too do “attractor” or “meaningful” ideas bend the ideas around them to their will.

  

the science of this bending starts as psychology (as the substrate of meaning) but quickly gets abstracted into the interactions of the symbols themselves.

  

Thats why you can go from freud to jung to Thomas Aquinnas.

  

Psychology and Religion live on different levels of abstraction of this science of meaning.
