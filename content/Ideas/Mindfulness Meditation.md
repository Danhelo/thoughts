#self/meditation #mindfulness 

Pursuing [[Waking Up App]] approach, designed not to calm down or de stress, but to observe the nature of my own mind, to discover fundamental truths about it.

As they state, your mind is the fundamental truth of the universe, and the basis of every contribution of life. It makes sense to *train it*. We spend little time truly content and present in the moment. We fail to connect. Mindfulness corrects this.

Additionally, mindfulness can help you discover a sense of freedom that's always present in my mind for me to retreat to. 

Main technique taught is [[Vipassana]] (insight) meditation, coming from [[Buddhism]]. All I am asked to do is to notice what is already embedded in my moment to moment conscious experience. No need to create new

## Fundamentals:

It's about sharpening the tool of the mind. This is to not identify with the content of my thoughts, but the pure state of what my mind *actually* is (attention-allocating circuit). 

Sam: Why meditate? The quality of my life depends on the quality of my mind. Everything good or bad appears in consciousness to matter. Therefore we can make the best of bad situations. Changing your response to the world ~~ Changing the world in actuality (good luck doing that lol). Seize to respond to life in ways that needlessly cause suffering.

We fail to notice how our minds work. For instance, every instance of thought (good or bad) that has ever gone through me has vanished eventually.
