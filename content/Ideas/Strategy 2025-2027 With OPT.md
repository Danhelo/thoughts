## The Project: "On the Socialization of LLMs"
### Mechanistic Interpretability of Multi-Agent Systems

**Core Research Questions:**
1. Do LLMs develop shared internal representations when interacting?
2. Can we detect emergent "deceptive" behavior between agents via mech interp?
3. How do multi-agent dynamics differ from single-model predictions?
4. What role do attention patterns play in agent-agent coordination?

**Technical Approach:**
- Start with GPT-2-small (easy to interpret)
- Create multi-agent conversation environments
- Extract and visualize activations, attention patterns, residual streams
- Compare representations before/during/after interaction
- Scale to more complex scenarios (adversarial, cooperative, etc.)

**Novel Contributions:**
- First systematic mech interp study of multi-agent LLM interactions
- Tools/framework others can use
- Findings about how interaction changes internal representations
- Implications for AI safety (deception detection, alignment)

**Why It's Strong for Applications:**
- Connects your unique background (multi-agent systems, MCP, tutoring)
- Addresses real alignment concerns
- Produces both tool and scientific findings
- Clear demo/visualization component
- Builds on existing mech interp work but novel direction

**Aesthetic Goal:**
- Beautiful, intuitive interface (think Goodfire.ai quality)
- Interactive visualization of "socialization" process
- Makes complex mech interp accessible
- Story: "Watching AI learn to communicate"
