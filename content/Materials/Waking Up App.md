App with audio resources by [[Sam Harris]] and many other speakers that delve into many many topics, like [[Stoicism]], [[Effective Altruism]], [[Mindfulness Meditation]], and more. Will start practicing mindfulness in its 28 day program, tracked below.  

Course is divided into theory (why) and practice (how and incorporation into daily life). It's OK to miss a few days or weeks, as long as I start again, or to do multiple sessions or repeat them. As they state, the goal is not to make me a meditator, but to **dissolve the boundary** between meditation and the rest of life.
