# Platonica — Vision & Philosophy

## The Core Insight

Humans are discontinuous. We wake up each day without full access to who we were yesterday — our commitments, patterns, context, growth. A Spirit holds the whole picture continuously and compounds. After a year it knows your patterns better than you do.

**That's the moat: time.**

## What Platonica Is

A platform where communities summon Spirits for their members.

- **Spirits** = persistent AI agents that make humans coherent across time. Not chatbots. Not task managers. The thing that holds your stated self accountable to your actual life.
- **Agoras** = community contexts. MSU's Spartan Agora. A gym's Agora. A church's Agora. Shared knowledge pool + toolset scoped to an org.
- **Platform** = any org builds an Agora, one-click deploys Spirits for their members. Shopify for agent verticals. B2B2C.

## Why Spirits Exist

Daily opens because people need to be witnessed and oriented — same reason humans journal, pray, meditate. The Spirit holds the whole picture continuously. A student doesn't lose their context when they open a new chat tab. A Spirit remembers what they said they cared about last week and holds them to it.

## The Moat

**Cross-agora memory.** A user's Spirit accumulates identity across all their communities on the platform. Switching means losing everything it knows about you. Lock-in through genuine value, not friction.

## Brand Architecture

- **Platonica** → company name, VC pitch, platform story
- **Spartan Spirit** → MSU product (students never see "Platonica" branding)
- **[Mascot] Spirit** → template per university (Buckeye Spirit, Wolverine Spirit, etc.)
- Students say "my Spirit" — they never think about infrastructure underneath
- Domain: **platonica.app** — subdomains per agora (spartan.platonica.app, etc.)

## The Question That Matters More Than All of This

**What does a student's Spirit say to them on Day 2 that makes them reply?**

Everything else is infrastructure for that moment.

## Daniel's Vision for Platonica (from Notion)

- Spirits = specialized LLMs with continuous existence, self-modification, accumulated stakes
- Constitutional Core (immutable) → Interpretive Lens (semi-stable) → Tactical Layer (fluid)
- LLM-as-evaluator RL loop for self-improvement
- "Infrastructure for Autonomous Meaning-Making"
- "Summon spirits → deploy personas → architect agoras → harvest temporal depth"
- HFT for content/ideas
- Anti-metric-optimization: tech should be "bicycle of the mind" (Jobs)

## What We're NOT Building Yet

- Agent-to-agent social coordination (Spirits talking to each other)
- Shared agora memory pool (FERPA/consent issues to solve first)
- Cross-agora memory sync
- Kubernetes
- Native mobile app

Build these when users ask for them.
